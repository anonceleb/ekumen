Glossary

Terms, acronyms and charge codes used across Indian freight forwarding. Fees, thresholds and specific rates change often — verify anything you plan to act on against the current circular or notification.

AD Code
Authorised Dealer code of the exporter's bank, registered per port. Without it a shipping bill cannot be filed at that location.
AEO
Authorised Economic Operator — a graduated compliance status (Tiers T1/T2/T3 for traders, LO for logistics operators) that trades a clean track record for faster clearance and reduced bank guarantee requirements.
AMS / ISF / ENS / AFR
Advance manifest filings required before loading, specific to the destination country — AMS/ISF for the US, ENS for the EU, AFR for Japan. Late or wrong filing draws penalties.
AWB
Air Waybill — the air freight transport document. Unlike a bill of lading, it is not a document of title; cargo is released to the named consignee on identification.
BAF / FAF
Bunker / Fuel Adjustment Factor — a fuel cost pass-through revised periodically by carriers.
Bill of Entry (BoE)
The import declaration filed with Indian Customs. Types: home consumption (duty paid now), warehousing/into-bond (duty deferred), ex-bond (clearing previously warehoused goods).
Bill of Lading (BL)
The ocean transport document. It performs three functions: receipt for the goods, evidence of the contract of carriage, and — when made "to order" and endorsed — document of title.
Blank sailing
A scheduled sailing cancelled by the carrier to manage capacity.
Bonded warehouse
A customs-licensed facility where imported goods are stored with duty deferred until they leave the warehouse.
Breakbulk
Non-containerised general cargo loaded piece by piece, typically project or oversized cargo.
BRC / e-BRC
Bank Realisation Certificate — evidence that export proceeds were realised, required for incentive claims.
CAROTAR
Customs (Administration of Rules of Origin under Trade Agreements) Rules, 2020 — places the burden of holding origin evidence on the Indian importer claiming a duty preference, and lets customs verify the claim with the exporting country's issuing authority.
CASS
Cargo Accounts Settlement System — IATA's centralised settlement mechanism between airlines and accredited cargo agents, on a fixed periodic schedule.
CB / CHA
Customs Broker, formerly Custom House Agent — the only party legally permitted to transact customs clearance business on behalf of an importer or exporter, licensed under CBLR 2018.
CBM
Cubic metre — the volume unit used for LCL pricing.
CFS
Container Freight Station — an off-dock, customs-bonded area attached to a gateway port for stuffing, destuffing, examination and storage. Clearance is against the gateway port, not the CFS itself.
Chargeable weight
The greater of actual gross weight and volumetric (dimensional) weight — the basis air freight is billed on.
Claused BL
A bill of lading noting visible damage or defect. Rejected under most letters of credit, which require a "clean" bill.
Co-loader
A consolidator that sells container or ULD space to other forwarders rather than directly to shippers.
Consignee
The party to whom goods are shipped, named on the transport document.
Consolidation
Combining multiple shippers' cargo into a single container (ocean) or ULD (air).
DAP / DPU / DDP
Delivered Incoterms in which the seller bears risk all the way to (or past) destination. See Incoterms 2020 in practice.
Demurrage
Charge by the terminal or CFS (the custodian) for cargo or a container occupying their ground beyond the free period — a real-estate charge.
Detention
Charge by the shipping line for a container held outside the terminal beyond free time — an equipment rental charge.
DG
Dangerous Goods, classified under the IMDG Code for sea or IATA DGR for air. Mis-declaration is a safety issue, not a paperwork one.
DGFT
Directorate General of Foreign Trade — administers the IEC, the Foreign Trade Policy, and export incentive schemes.
DO
Delivery Order — the carrier's authorisation for the terminal or CFS to release cargo, issued against BL surrender, payment of local charges, and any required deposit.
DPD / DPE
Direct Port Delivery (import) / Direct Port Entry (export) — moving a container directly between the terminal and the importer or exporter's premises, bypassing the CFS. See DPD vs CFS.
Drawback
Refund of customs duty paid on imported inputs used in goods that are subsequently exported.
DSO
Days Sales Outstanding — the average time to collect a receivable. A core working-capital metric in a business that pays carriers before it collects from customers.
eBL
Electronic bill of lading — a digitally native version of the BL, legally enabled by jurisdictions adopting the UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records.
EGM
Export General Manifest, filed by the carrier. Must match the shipping bill before duty drawback or other export incentives disburse.
e-Sanchit
The paperless upload facility on ICEGATE for supporting documents attached to a customs declaration.
e-Way Bill
The electronic document required for road or rail movement of goods above a value threshold under GST, for the domestic leg of both imports and exports.
EXW
Ex Works — the Incoterm placing minimum obligation on the seller. Commonly misused for exports, since the buyer usually cannot file an Indian shipping bill.
FCL / LCL
Full Container Load / Less than Container Load — whether a shipment fills a container on its own or shares one with other shippers' cargo.
FEU / TEU
Forty-foot / Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit — the standard container size units.
FOB
Free On Board — the dominant Incoterm in Indian exports, under which the overseas buyer controls (and chooses) the freight.
Free time
The number of days allowed before demurrage or detention begins to accrue. Negotiable, and often worth more to a customer than a small rate discount.
GRI
General Rate Increase — a carrier-announced, across-the-board freight rate increase, usually with advance notice.
GTA
Goods Transport Agency — the GST category for road transporters issuing a consignment note, with specific reverse-charge treatment.
HAWB / MAWB
House / Master Air Waybill — the forwarder's contract with its customer, and the airline's contract with the consolidator, respectively.
HBL / MBL
House / Master Bill of Lading — the forwarder's contract with its customer, and the shipping line's contract with the booking party, respectively.
HS Code / ITC(HS)
The Harmonised System classification used to determine duty rates and restrictions. India uses an 8-digit ITC(HS) code.
IATA
International Air Transport Association — accredits cargo agents, sets dangerous goods regulations, and operates the CASS settlement system.
ICD
Inland Container Depot — a customs-notified inland facility with full clearance powers, legally treated as a port.
ICEGATE
The Indian Customs Electronic Gateway — the front-end portal for filing declarations, payments, document upload and status enquiry.
IEC
Importer Exporter Code — a ten-digit, PAN-based code from DGFT, mandatory for any commercial import or export, and requiring annual reconfirmation on the DGFT portal.
IGM
Import General Manifest, filed by the carrier before vessel arrival. Each bill of lading becomes a line item that the Bill of Entry references.
Incoterms
ICC rules that allocate cost, risk and obligation — but not title or payment terms — between a buyer and seller. See Incoterms 2020 in practice.
KYC
Know Your Customer — verification of a client's identity and address, a specific regulatory obligation for customs brokers.
LC
Letter of Credit, governed by UCP 600. The issuing bank pays against documents, not goods, so a single inconsistency between documents is a "discrepancy" the bank can refuse.
LEO
Let Export Order — Customs' authorisation to load cargo for export, issued after assessment and any required examination.
MTD / MTO
Multimodal Transport Document / Operator — a single document covering carriage across two or more modes door to door, issuable only by an operator registered with DG Shipping.
NVOCC
Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier — issues its own bills of lading and buys space from vessel operators, acting as a carrier without owning ships.
ODC / OOG
Over-Dimensional Cargo / Out of Gauge — cargo exceeding standard container dimensions, requiring flat racks or open-top equipment and special permits.
OOC
Out of Charge — Customs' release order on an import, the import-side equivalent of the LEO.
PGA
Partner Government Agency — bodies such as FSSAI, Plant Quarantine or BIS whose clearance a shipment may require, routed through the SWIFT single window on ICEGATE.
Pure agent
A GST concept (Rule 33) permitting exclusion of certain pass-through costs from taxable value, under strict conditions that most forwarders' pricing structures do not actually meet.
Reefer
A refrigerated container, used for temperature-controlled cargo such as seafood, produce or pharmaceuticals.
RMS
Risk Management System — Customs' automated engine that routes each declaration to facilitation, assessment or physical examination.
Rollover
Cargo that misses its booked vessel and is shifted to a later sailing.
SCOMET
Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies — India's dual-use export control list, requiring DGFT authorisation.
SDR
Special Drawing Right — the IMF unit of account used to express carrier liability limits under international carriage conventions.
Sea Waybill
A non-negotiable ocean transport document. Cargo is released to the named consignee on identification, with no original required.
SEZ
Special Economic Zone — supplies into an SEZ are treated as exports and move under a separate Bill of Entry/Bill of Export framework.
Shipping Bill
The Indian export declaration, filed on ICEGATE, carrying the Let Export Order once granted.
SI
Shipping Instructions — the bill of lading particulars (shipper, consignee, marks, weights, freight terms) submitted to the carrier ahead of the documentation cut-off.
Steamer agent
A shipping line's local representative, who books, issues bills of lading, and files the IGM/EGM on the line's behalf.
Stuffing / destuffing
Loading or unloading cargo into or out of a shipping container.
Switch BL
A replacement bill of lading set, typically used in cross-trade to substitute a new set of parties for the original — legitimate but abuse-prone.
Telex release
Release of cargo at destination without an original bill of lading, after the originals have been surrendered at origin. Irreversible once given.
THC
Terminal Handling Charge — the terminal's charge for moving a container between quay and yard, billed by the carrier at both ends of the voyage.
Transhipment
Transfer of cargo from one vessel to another at an intermediate port, adding transit time and rollover risk.
ULD
Unit Load Device — an air cargo container or pallet used to consolidate shipments for loading.
VGM
Verified Gross Mass — the shipper's legally mandated declaration of a packed container's weight under SOLAS, without which a carrier cannot load it.
W/M
Weight or Measurement — the greater of weight in tonnes or volume in cubic metres, the standard basis for LCL and breakbulk pricing.
