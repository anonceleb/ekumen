DPD vs CFS

Once an import container clears customs at an Indian port, it can leave the terminal in one of two ways. Which one applies is one of the biggest single drivers of import cost and speed.

Direct Port Delivery (DPD) lets an eligible importer take the container straight from the terminal to its own premises, without routing it through a Container Freight Station. It exists specifically to cut dwell time and cost.

The alternative, the CFS route, sends the container to an off-dock Container Freight Station for destuffing, storage, and — if flagged — examination, before cargo is released.

DimensionDPDCFS route
Who qualifiesImporters approved by Customs, typically AEO or high-volume compliant importers, registered for DPD with the shipping line in advanceAnyone
SpeedThe box can leave the terminal within hours of Out of ChargeTypically adds one to several days
CostLower — avoids CFS handling, ground rent, destuffingCFS charges are a large, and often opaque, cost block
ObligationMust evacuate quickly, or penalties or a shift back to CFS can applyLonger free storage, more relaxed
Best forFCL, single-consignee cargo, an importer with its own transport and a ready warehouseLCL, multi-consignee boxes, cargo needing examination, importers without immediate storage

Why this decision compounds

The choice isn't purely operational. DPD eligibility rewards exactly the kind of compliance record that Authorised Economic Operator status also rewards — a clean filing history, predictable volume, an importer Customs already trusts. The CFS route, by contrast, is where most of the friction in Indian import clearance actually accumulates: slab-based storage charges that escalate the longer a box sits, and the destination handling costs that are the least visible part of a quote to the end customer.

For an LCL shipment or anything likely to be flagged for examination, the CFS route isn't really optional — DPD generally isn't offered for multi-consignee boxes. The decision that matters is whether an FCL importer with DPD eligibility is actually using it, or defaulting to CFS out of habit.
